# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-28

- Initial release, adapted from hinge-profile-optimizer 1.2.1 (MIT).
- Bumble product facts as of September 2026: 300-character bio, three 160-character prompts, six photos, filterable badges, up to five interests, up to two dating intentions, photo verification.
- Messaging rules as of August 2026: anyone can message first, 72-hour window each side, Opening Moves retired, low-effort openers flagged.
- Audit weighting and completeness checklist rebuilt around badges and filters.
- Copy phase assigns a job to each slot (bio for warmth and hook; prompts for filter, reveal, opener) and adds the first-message test.
- Photo phase adds card-crop rules and photo-plus-prompt pairing in place of captions.
- Settings reference covers Boost, Premium, and Premium+ tiers, Notes, SuperSwipe, Spotlight, Beeline, Snooze, Incognito, and Travel Mode.
- Packaged for Claude Code, Codex, and the OpenAI Plugins Directory with a buildable archive.
