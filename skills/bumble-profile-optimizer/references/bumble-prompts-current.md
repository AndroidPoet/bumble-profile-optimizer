# Bumble Prompts - Current Options

**IMPORTANT:** Bumble updates prompt options regularly, and in 2026 it is rolling out a new profile layout ("chapters") in select markets. Always verify current options with the user.

**Ask:** "Can you tell me what prompt options you see in the app? Or screenshot the prompt selection screen?"

---

## The Text Slots (verify in app)

| Slot | Limit | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Bio ("About me") | 300 characters | Free text. Shows above the prompts when someone scrolls the card. Bumble's own guidance: 1-3 sentences of specific detail. |
| Prompts | Up to 3, from 40+ options | 160 characters per answer (some sources say 120 — check the counter in the app). A prompt can be paired with a photo. Once added, a prompt can be swapped but the slot can't be removed. |
| Photo captions | None | Bumble photos have no captions. Pair a photo with a prompt if it needs context. |

Total text budget: roughly 780 characters. Everything else on the profile is a badge.

## Prompt Selection Strategy

You get three. Use them for three different jobs:

| Job | Best prompt types | Why |
|-----|-------------------|-----|
| **Filter** | "My most controversial opinion is", "A non-negotiable", "Swipe right if", "We'll get along if" | Strong opinions sort the pool (Fiore & Donath, 2005). Scale the edge to market thickness. |
| **Reveal** | "I get way too excited about", "My simple pleasures", "My hidden gem in the city", "My after-work ritual" | Specific, moderate-depth disclosure is the sweet spot (Gibbs et al., 2006). Great for cultural markers. |
| **Opener bait** | "I'll never stop talking about", "The most embarrassing song on my playlist", "Two truths and a lie", "Perfect first date" | Hands the match a first message. On Bumble an unopened match expires in 72 hours. |

**Don't spend all three on the same job.** Three opinions reads as combative. Three reveals reads as a diary. One of each, plus a bio that carries the warmth, is the shape.

### Prompts to Use Carefully

**"I'm looking for"** - The dating-intention badge already answers this. Only use it if the answer is specific and says something about them, not just about the relationship.

**"My love language is"** - Almost always generic. Skip unless the answer is a joke that lands.

**"Two truths and a lie"** - Overused, only if the items are genuinely surprising and at least one is an opener.

**"A cause I care about"** - Real warmth signal if it's specific and they actually do something about it. Reads as performative if it's a category.

**"Swipe right if"** - Bumble's card language. Works as a filter line; fails as a list of demands.

### Pairing a Prompt With a Photo

Bumble lets you attach a photo to a prompt answer. Use it when a photo needs the context a caption would have given on another app:

- The venue photo + "My hidden gem in the city"
- The activity photo + "I get way too excited about"
- The group photo where they're hard to spot + a prompt that says which one they are, with a joke

## What Replaced Opening Moves

Opening Moves (a preset question a match could answer to start the chat) were retired in August 2026 when Bumble dropped the women-message-first rule. Now either person sends the first message and each side has 72 hours. If the user's app still shows an Opening Move slot, their version hasn't updated: treat it as one extra opener-bait line, under 100 characters, phrased as a question they'd actually enjoy answering.

## Verification Script

When starting, ask:

> "Before we write anything, can you check what prompt options are available in your app, and what the character counter says? The exact prompts change, and Bumble is mid-redesign, so I want to make sure we're working with what you actually have.
>
> Either tell me the ones that appeal to you, or screenshot the prompt selection screen."

This ensures recommendations match actual available options.
